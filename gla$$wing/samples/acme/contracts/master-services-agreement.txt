Supplier: Acme Field Services

Master Services Agreement
Buyer: Northline Industrial
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Contract year: anniversary of the effective date
Currency: USD

1. Appointment
Acme Field Services provides maintenance, calibration, and on-site repair labor to Northline plants in Ohio and Ontario. This agreement covers services only. Parts and materials are billed separately and sit outside the labor rate card.

2. Ordering
Buyer authorizes work by written statement of work. Each statement of work names the site, the scope, the rate, and the window. Acme confirms within two business days.

3. Fees and invoicing
Acme invoices monthly in arrears for labor performed. Each invoice states the statement of work, the technician hours, and the applicable rate. A charge above the rate card is a leak against that line.

4. Rate card
Standard technician labor is billed at $95.00 per hour. Emergency call-out labor is billed at $150.00 per hour. Travel is billed at $0.65 per mile.

5. Service levels
Acme responds to a priority-one outage within four hours and restores service within twenty-four hours. A quarter that falls below a 95 percent on-time response rate is a service-level miss.

6. Confidentiality
Site drawings, rates, and incident reports are confidential. Neither party publishes the other's information.

7. Liability
Acme's liability for defective work is limited to re-performance of that work or a credit for the labor charged for it. This cap does not apply to injury or to damage caused by Acme's gross negligence.

8. Term and termination
The primary term runs three years from the effective date. Either party may terminate for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days after written notice.

9. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. This section is entire agreement boilerplate and carries no commercial obligation.
