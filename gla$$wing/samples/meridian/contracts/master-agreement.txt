Supplier: Meridian Components

Master Supply Agreement
Buyer: Northline Industrial
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Contract year: anniversary of the effective date
Currency: USD

1. Appointment
Meridian Components supplies machined parts, seals, sensors, and small motors to Northline plants in Ohio and Ontario. This agreement covers goods only. Freight and tax are outside the rebate base.

2. Ordering
Buyer releases purchases by written schedule. Each release states the part, quantity, and requested week. Meridian confirms within two business days.

3. Efforts during shortage
Meridian shall use commercially reasonable efforts to prioritize Northline during an allocation or shortage of raw material.

4. Price schedule
Unit prices are stated in the attached price schedule. A shipment above the stated unit price is a leak against that part.

5. Quality
Parts must meet the drawing revision on the release. Buyer may reject a lot that fails incoming inspection and return it at Meridian's cost.

6. Tooling
Buyer-owned tools stay at Meridian. Meridian shall not use those tools for another customer without written consent.

7. Forecast
Buyer provides a rolling six-month forecast. The first eight weeks are firm. Later weeks are for capacity planning.

8. Packaging
Each carton shows the part number, lot, and quantity. Mixed lots in one carton are not accepted.

9. Liability
Meridian's liability for a defective part is limited to repair, replacement, or a credit for the purchase price of that part. This liability clause does not cap a recall caused by Meridian's process.

10. Termination
Either party may terminate for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days after written notice. Termination does not erase rebates already earned.

11. Records
Meridian keeps shipment and price records for four years. Buyer may review those records once each contract year.

12. Insurance
Meridian maintains general liability coverage of not less than $5,000,000 and names Buyer as an additional insured.

13. Confidentiality
Drawings and pricing are confidential. Neither party publishes the other's prices.

14. Assignment
Meridian may not assign this agreement without Buyer's consent, except to a successor of the whole business.
