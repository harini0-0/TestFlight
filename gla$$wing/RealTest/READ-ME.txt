Vendor: Insight Public Sector, Inc.
Buyer framework: Texas Department of Information Resources
Contract: DIR-CPO-5239 (Adobe and Microsoft software)
Term: starts 10/05/2023, renews through 10/05/2027, expiration 10/05/2029
Every order and quote below cites DIR-CPO-5239.

Load 01-contract-rules as the rule documents.
Load 02-orders-and-quotes as the transactions to check against those rules.

Rules that should come out of the contract set
- Appendix C: Adobe customer discount is 6% off the publisher discount level.
  Volume floors: CLP Education 100,000+ points; CLP Government 300,000+ points.
  Microsoft Enterprise Agreement / Select Plus customer discount is 16.50%.
  Every quoted price must include the DIR administrative fee of 0.75% of the customer price.
- Contract: initial term up to 2 years, then automatic renewal unless notice is given at least 60 days before the renewal date.
- Appendix A: correct invoices are due 30 calendar days after goods, services, or a correct invoice, whichever is later.
  Invoices must match the purchase order.
- Amendment 1 changes a compliance clause only. Pricing and payment terms stay in force.

Check to run
List x 0.94 x 1.0075 is the 6% discount with the 0.75% fee included.
Grand Prairie and the PUCT order match that formula on the Acrobat and Creative Cloud lines.
Fort Bend states 6% off list, but the unit prices are well below that floor (Acrobat $130.90 vs list $286.00; All Apps $1,529.43 vs list $1,831.00). That is a deeper discount than the contract minimum, and the stated 6% does not reconcile to the unit price.
