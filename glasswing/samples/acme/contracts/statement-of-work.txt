Supplier: Acme Field Services

Statement of Work - Turbine Line Calibration
Buyer: Northline Industrial
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Currency: USD

1. Incorporation
This Statement of Work is issued under and incorporated into the Master Services Agreement. Where this document is silent, the Master Services Agreement controls. Where a term here conflicts with the Master Services Agreement, this Statement of Work controls for the work described below only.

2. Scope
Acme calibrates the turbine line sensors at the Ohio plant on a monthly cadence for the 2026 contract year. Each visit covers up to forty sensors and produces a signed calibration report per the checklist in Exhibit A.

3. Rate
Labor for this work is billed at the standard technician rate in Section 4 of the Master Services Agreement. Emergency call-outs requested by Buyer are billed at the emergency rate in that same section.

4. Invoicing
Acme invoices this work under Section 3 of the Master Services Agreement, itemized by visit and technician hours. Materials consumed during calibration are billed separately and are not part of the labor rate card.

5. Service level
The response and restoration commitments in Section 5 of the Master Services Agreement, measured each quarter, apply to any outage of the turbine line discovered during a calibration visit.

6. Liability
The liability cap in Section 7 of the Master Services Agreement, and only that cap, applies to the work in this Statement of Work. Nothing in this Statement of Work raises that cap.

7. Term
This Statement of Work runs for the 2026 contract year and expires with the primary term of the Master Services Agreement unless renewed in writing.
